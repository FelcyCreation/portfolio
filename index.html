Meet The Designer

"Clothing is the art you wear to tell your own story."

Felcy is a passionate women's fashion designer who transforms sketches and premium fabrics into comfortable, show-stopping garments. From festive lehengas and royal sarees to intricately styled blouses and modern kurtis, every creation is hand-crafted to celebrate individuality.

Through her popular YouTube channel Stitch & Style by Felcy, she shares her craft with thousands of aspiring tailors and fashion enthusiasts, proving that true style lies in meticulous attention to detail.
